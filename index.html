How to play

Goal: paint every open floor tile. Grey blocks are solid and never need paint.

Move: press an arrow button, an arrow key or W/A/S/D, or swipe the board. The ball rolls in that direction until it hits a wall, painting every tile it crosses. It cannot stop part-way, so plan each roll. Dimmed arrows are walled off from where the ball sits.

Assists: Undo (U) takes back a roll, Hint (H) suggests one, Restart (R) replays this maze, New maze draws another. Fewer moves than par is a good score.
